🦦 Rheo Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 20, 2026

📋 Data We Collect

Rheo uses only locally stored data on your device to improve your experience:

  • ELO score and ranking information
  • Quiz statistics (correct/incorrect counts)
  • Daily goals and streak information
  • Selected avatar and username
  • Preferred programming language

Your learning data is stored locally on your device. To improve stability and the learning experience, the app also sends anonymous usage analytics and crash diagnostics to Firebase (see “Third-Party Services” below). This never includes your name, email address, or any content you type.

🤖 Question Content

Questions and lessons come from a built-in question bank that ships with the app.

  • No code you write or read is sent to any external service.
  • No account or personal information is required to use the questions.
  • All learning content works fully offline.

🔒 Data Security

  • All user data is stored locally and encrypted on your device (Hive database).
  • The app does not require a user account or registration.
  • Data deletion: you can erase all your data anytime via Profile → Settings → "Reset All Progress". Uninstalling the app also removes everything, since all data is stored locally on your device.

🔗 Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Analytics: Anonymous usage analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Crash reporting

These services have their own privacy policies available on their respective websites.

👶 Audience & Children's Privacy

Rheo is intended for a general audience (ages 12 and up). The app:

  • Does not collect personally identifying information (name, email, contacts, or precise location)
  • Does not display ads
  • Does not offer real-money in-app purchases (gems and rewards are earned in-game only)
  • Has no social media login or messaging features
