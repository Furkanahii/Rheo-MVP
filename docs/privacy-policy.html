🦦 Rheo Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 20, 2026

📋 Data We Collect

Rheo uses only locally stored data on your device to improve your experience:

  • ELO score and ranking information
  • Quiz statistics (correct/incorrect counts)
  • Daily goals and streak information
  • Selected avatar and username
  • Preferred programming language

This data is stored ONLY on your device and is never sent to any server.

🤖 AI Question Generation

The AI-powered question generation feature uses the Google Gemini API.

  • Only question generation requests are sent to the API.
  • Your personal information is never sent to the API.
  • The static question bank (752+ questions) works without the API.

🔒 Data Security

  • All user data is stored locally and encrypted on your device (Hive database).
  • The app does not require a user account or registration.
  • Data deletion: You can delete all your data via Settings > Reset Progress.

🔗 Third-Party Services

  • Google Gemini API: AI question generation (optional)
  • Firebase Analytics: Anonymous usage analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Crash reporting

These services have their own privacy policies available on their respective websites.

👶 Children's Privacy

Rheo is suitable for all ages. The app:

  • Does not collect personal information
  • Does not display ads
  • Does not include in-app purchases
  • Has no social media integration
